SAFETY AND COMMUNICATION

Good care begins before the booking.

Before agreeing to babysit, I'll ask parents to explain their children's routines and anything important I need to know. We'll talk through expectations so everyone understands the plan.

01

Talk through the details

Children's ages, routines, allergies, emergency contacts and house rules.

02

Be clear about experience

I'll be honest about my training and experience and answer reasonable questions.

03

Agree the plan

Confirm timing, address, payment, parent check-ins and what to do if plans change.
